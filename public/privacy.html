privacy

bene vixit, bene qui latuit. we sell privacy as a way of living well, so this page is short and true.

no accounts

there is nothing to sign up for. we do not have usernames, passwords, profiles, or a database of who you are.

the conversation at the door

your exchange with the keeper is processed to decide whether the door opens, then deleted — transcripts are purged when you pass or within about an hour of going quiet, whichever comes first. we do not keep them, train on them, or tie them to you.

what infrastructure sees

the site runs on cloudflare. cloudflare turnstile checks that you are a person, and cloudflare's systems handle requests the way any host does. we keep aggregate operational logs (error rates, model usage) without profiles of visitors.

if you buy something

payment is zcash via cipherpay, which is non-custodial — we never see a wallet balance, an identity, or anything beyond confirmation that the payment cleared. we need a shipping address to send you a physical thing; addresses are purged from our systems after fulfillment. use whatever name pleases you.

cookies

a single session cookie may carry your entry pass to checkout. no analytics cookies, no tracking pixels, no third-party beacons.
