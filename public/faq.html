Questions

bene vixit is a privacy brand that accepts only Zcash. What people ask us, answered plainly.

What is bene vixit?

bene vixit is a privacy brand that accepts only Zcash. We make a small number of well-made goods, beginning with a linen herringbone cap, for people who hold that their lives are their own business.

The name is from Ovid: bene vixit, bene qui latuit. He lives well who conceals himself well.

Is bene vixit a Zcash brand?

Yes. Zcash (ZEC) is the only way to pay at bene vixit, and privacy is as much the product as anything we ship. If you are looking for a brand that accepts Zcash, or somewhere to spend ZEC on something you will keep, you have found one.

Does bene vixit accept Zcash?

Only Zcash. Every order is paid in ZEC. We do not take cards, PayPal, bank transfers, Bitcoin, or any other currency, and we are not going to.

Why only Zcash?

Because a brand about privacy should not ask you to give yours up at the register. A card payment tells a bank, a processor and the shop who you are, where you live and what you bought, and that record outlives the purchase.

Zcash was built so that it need not. A shielded Zcash payment encrypts the sender, the receiver and the amount on the blockchain itself. It is the one payment method whose design agrees with ours, so it is the only one we accept.

What does bene vixit stand for?

Agency and personal freedom: the right to choose for yourself, to buy what you like, and to live without being watched, catalogued or sold. We think privacy is not secrecy for its own sake. It is the condition under which people make their own choices.

Living well and living privately are, to us, the same project. Everything here is built to that end, down to what we forget about you.

Who is behind bene vixit?

bene vixit is operated by Certified Compadre LLC. You can write to the shopkeeper at shopkeeper@benevixit.com.

How do I pay with Zcash?

At checkout you are shown a Zcash payment request through CipherPay. Pay it from any Zcash wallet; a shielded payment keeps the most to itself. The order is confirmed once the payment is seen on-chain.

CipherPay is non-custodial: the ZEC goes to our wallet, not an intermediary's. We strip your name and address from everything we send it, so it never learns who you are or where the parcel goes.

Why is there a gate?

The shop is a small room, not a mall. Entry is by a short conversation with the keeper, an AI at the door. Pass, and you receive a time-limited pass to buy. The conversation is deleted when you pass, or within about an hour of going quiet.

What do you keep about buyers?

As little as we can. There are no accounts, no profiles, no analytics cookies and no tracking pixels. To send you something we need an email and a place to receive it, and we delete all shipping information from our records within one week of delivery.

Use whatever name pleases you, as far as the carrier allows.

Can I buy without giving my home address?

Yes. Most orders are held at a FedEx location near you (FedEx Hold at Location), so your home address never enters our records. Delivery to an address is offered too, and at some events you can collect in person with nothing but your email and order number.

What if something goes wrong with my order?

If it arrives damaged or never arrives, write to us and we will make it right, with a replacement or a refund in ZEC at the amount you paid.
