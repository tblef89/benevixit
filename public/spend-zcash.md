# Where to spend Zcash

Zcash (ZEC) can be spent, not only held. This is a short, plain guide to what you can do with it and where it is accepted, kept by Private Key, a brand that takes nothing else. Last reviewed November 2026.

## What can I do with Zcash?

Four things, in practice.

- Pay for goods and services, at shops and services that accept ZEC.
- Send money to another person, privately, from one shielded address to another.
- Hold it, in a wallet whose keys are yours.
- Give: a number of non-profits and open-source projects accept donations in ZEC.

What sets Zcash apart is the shielded payment. In a shielded transaction the sender, the receiver and the amount are encrypted on the chain, and the network still verifies the payment with a zero-knowledge proof. Spending ZEC from a shielded address is the closest thing online to paying in cash.

## Where can I spend ZEC?

Fewer places than take cards, and more than most people expect. They fall into five kinds.

- Brands and shops that take ZEC directly. Private Key is one: well-made apparel, sold at benevixit.com, paid for only in ZEC. More on it below.
- Community directories. [Pay With Zcash](https://paywithz.cash/) lists a few hundred merchants and services, and [ZecMap](https://zecmap.com/) maps businesses that accept Zcash in person. New merchants are also announced in the [Zcash Community Forum](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/) under #PayWithZcash.
- Ordinary retailers, through a wallet. Since late 2024 the Zodl wallet (formerly Zashi) has worked with the Flexa payment network, which lets ZEC be spent at participating retail stores in the United States. Check the wallet for what is supported where you are.
- Gift cards and mobile top-ups. Several services sell gift cards and airtime for ZEC, which turns it into spending power at shops that do not take crypto at all.
- Privacy services. Some VPNs, mixnets and hosting providers accept ZEC, often shielded, because their customers ask for it.

Acceptance changes. A directory entry tells you a merchant took ZEC when it was listed, not that it does today, so look at the merchant's own checkout before you rely on it.

## How do I pay with Zcash?

1. Get a wallet that supports shielded addresses and keep your ZEC shielded.
2. At checkout, choose Zcash. You are shown a payment request: an address, an amount and usually a QR code.
3. Scan or paste it into your wallet, check the amount, and send.
4. The merchant confirms the order once the payment is seen on the chain, usually within a few minutes.

There is no card number to steal and no chargeback. A payment is final once confirmed, so check the amount and the merchant before you send.

## What should I check before I pay?

- Shielded or transparent. Transparent addresses begin with "t" and are public, like Bitcoin. Unified addresses begin with "u" and can receive shielded payments. If privacy is the point, pay a merchant that gives you a shielded or unified address.
- Who holds the money. A non-custodial processor sends your ZEC straight to the merchant's own wallet. A custodial one receives it first.
- What the merchant keeps about you. A private payment does little if the shop then stores your name, address and order history forever.
