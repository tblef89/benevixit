# Zcash: where it came from

## The Zerocash research
Zcash grew out of academic research. The Zerocash paper, published in 2014 by
cryptographers including Eli Ben-Sasson, Alessandro Chiesa, Christina Garman,
Matthew Green, Ian Miers, Eran Tromer and Madars Virza, showed how a
Bitcoin-like currency could hide the sender, receiver and amount of a payment
using zero-knowledge proofs, while still letting everyone verify that no money
was created from nothing.

## Launch
Zcash launched on 28 October 2016. Like Bitcoin, its supply is capped at 21
million coins, and new coins are issued to miners on a schedule that halves
roughly every four years. The first halving was in November 2020 and the
second in November 2024.

## Who builds it
Zcash is developed in the open by several independent organisations. The
Electric Coin Company, founded by Zooko Wilcox, created Zcash. The Zcash
Foundation, a non-profit founded in 2017, also builds and stewards the
protocol. Neither is affiliated with Private Key.

## Block time
Since the Blossom upgrade in December 2019, a new Zcash block is produced
about every 75 seconds, half the original 150 seconds.
