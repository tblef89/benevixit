# Viewing keys and memos

## Viewing keys: disclosure by choice
A viewing key lets someone see a shielded address's activity without being
able to spend from it. The owner decides who, if anyone, receives one: an
accountant, an auditor, a tax authority, a business partner. Privacy in
Zcash is not secrecy from everyone forever; it is the owner's control over
who sees what.

## Kinds of viewing key
An incoming viewing key shows the payments an address receives. A full
viewing key shows payments both received and sent. Neither can move funds;
only the spending key can.

## Encrypted memos
Every shielded payment can carry an encrypted memo of up to 512 bytes. Only
the recipient can read it. People use it for invoice numbers, notes, or a
line of thanks, the way one might write on the back of a cheque, except that
no one else can read it.
